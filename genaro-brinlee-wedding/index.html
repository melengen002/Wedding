HOW WE GOT HERE

A pretty good
love story.

It started with a conversation that lasted longer than either of us expected. Genaro thought he was being charming. Brinlee is still deciding.

Somewhere between spontaneous drives, late-night food runs, meeting each other's families, and taking an unreasonable number of photos, we realized ordinary days were a lot better together.

A few years, plenty of inside jokes, and one very important question later, we're getting married. We can't wait to celebrate with the people who helped make our story what it is.

GOOD TO KNOW

Questions? We got you.

What should I wear?+

Formal attire for the reception. Think suits, dresses, and warm earth tones. Please save white for the bride.

Can I bring a plus one?+

If a plus one is included with your invitation, absolutely. Our RSVP will make it clear.

Are children invited?+

We love your little ones. Due to space, please bring only the guests named on your invitation.

What is an LDS temple sealing?+

In The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, a temple sealing is a sacred marriage ordinance centered on an eternal family covenant. Temple attendance is limited to invited members with current temple recommends, but we would love everyone to join us for the reception.

When should I RSVP?+

Please RSVP by May 1, 2027. Earlier is even better, because apparently weddings involve spreadsheets.
